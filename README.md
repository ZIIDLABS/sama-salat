## ✦ Welcome to Sama Salat

**Sama Salat** is a location-focused application that brings local weather information and daily Salat times together in one experience.

Created with Morocco in mind, the project aims to make two important parts of everyday life easier to access: understanding the weather around you and keeping track of your daily prayer schedule.

Simple in concept. Thoughtful in experience.

---

## ✦ The Experience

### 🌤 Local Weather

Stay connected to the weather around your location.

Get weather information designed to help you understand the conditions in your city and plan your day.

### 🕌 Daily Salat Times

Keep your daily prayer schedule accessible in one place.

Prayer times are connected to your selected location, making the experience relevant to your surroundings.

### 🇲🇦 Made for Morocco

From one city to another, Sama Salat is built around the idea of serving locations throughout Morocco.

A local experience, designed with local needs in mind.

## ✦ The Vision

Morocco is a country of different cities, landscapes, and everyday rhythms.

The weather changes from one place to another, and daily routines follow their own rhythm.

Sama Salat brings these elements together through a single, accessible experience.

Our goal is not to make things complicated.
